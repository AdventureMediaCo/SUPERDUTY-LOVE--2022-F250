SUPERDUTY LOVE- 2022 F250

Some times you just cant get enough of a certain build- and for us that's this head turning 2022 Ford Superduty sitting on a full Carli Suspension Unchained Kit with 38" Nitto tires on American Force Wheels. The truck features our Octane front LED style bumper loaded with 12 Baja Designs Squadron lights, as well as out Attitude series rear bumper. Both bumpers have been professionally color matched by the owner to give off that unique finish on this Superduty Platinum.

The Octane series front bumpers are one of our top seller for the Superduty's, and with this truck its clear as to why. These bumpers are sure to keep that low-profile clean look you want, with giving your truck that face lift of aggressive look in the front end. Let's also not forget that our rear Attitude series bumper also brings some nice features to the table such as rear corner bed steps, as well as a center step, and rear facing reverse lights.
